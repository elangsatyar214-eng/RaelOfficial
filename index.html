🌌 TWO UNIVERSES • 3 YEARS OF ETERNAL ORBIT 🌌 9 November • 3rd Anniversary

To my dearest Love, Rachel,

Happy 3rd Anniversary, my love. It feels like a quiet miracle how three whole years have unfolded so beautifully since November 9, 2023. A thousand days of shared glances, gentle laughter, warm conversations, and learning that the greatest, most fulfilling adventure of my life is simply loving you.

Three years together is not just a passage of time; it is a tapestry of everything we have weathered side by side. We have known the warmth of golden days when everything felt easy and bright, and we have walked through quiet storms, uncertainties, and the inevitable tides that tested our hearts. Yet with every ebb and flow of our love, we learned not to drift apart, but to hold each other a little closer, listen a little deeper, and choose each other with even greater certainty.

We came together as two distinct universes, whispering our prayers in different sanctuaries. Yet under the very same heavens, I know that God lovingly guided our orbits to collide and intertwine. Our differences were never distances to divide us; they became the quiet, tender harmony that makes us whole. In a world that never stops rushing and shifting, holding your hand has always felt like arriving home. You are my sanctuary, my calm, and my steadfast peace.

Thank you for walking this road with me through every season—through every joy, every trial, and every silent whisper in between. Thank you for your boundless patience, your gentle warmth, and for being the sweetest reason behind my smile every single day. Wherever tomorrow leads, I will always be right here beside you: to hold you, to celebrate you, to shelter you, and to love you unconditionally.

Through everything we have passed and all that lies ahead, what we share is not fleeting. Like the song playing softly between us, our love is etched into eternity. Ini abadi.

"After all this time? Always."

With all my love,

Elang
